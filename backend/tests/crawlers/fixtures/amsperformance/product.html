With 25 years of documented research and development in the forced induction field, AMS Performance has proven itself as the powerhouse in the automotive aftermarket. Our award winning performance systems are what all others are measured by.

The AMS Performance R8/Huracan Billet Rear Sway Bar End Links offer a huge improvement in rigidity over the weak OEM links. Where the factory links have deflection in their rubber bushings, the heim joints of the AMS Billet Rear Sway Bar End Links provide a solid connection, free of flex. Solid aluminum bushings and spherical bearings create a rigid link between the sway bar and knuckle.

  • Firms up Rear Suspension for Improved Stability and Reduced Understeer
  • Solid Bushing Design for Reduced Flex and Consistent Handling Characteristics
  • Spherical Bearing Allows Articulated Movement
  • 6061 T6 Billet Aluminum Construction
  • High Strength Alloy Steel PTFE Lined Rod Ends

Applications

  • 2017-Current Audi R8 (Incl. Plus & Performance models)
  • 2015-Current Lamborghini Huracan (Incl. Performante & Evo models)
